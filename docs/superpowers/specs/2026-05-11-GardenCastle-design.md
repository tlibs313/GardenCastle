# GardenCastle Design Specification

**Date:** 2026-05-11
**Project:** GardenCastle
**Genre:** Roguelike Modular Tower Defense (Static Action-TD)
**Aesthetic:** Whimsical Splatter (Cute vs. Gory)

## 1. Core Vision
"The Gardener" must protect and grow a series of "Objective Plants" in a central garden while defending against swarms of cute but deadly pests. The game combines the modular upgrade system of *Mech Assemble* with traditional grid-based tower defense and a "harvest-based" victory condition.

## 2. Gameplay Loop
1. **The Wave (Tactical):** 
   - Pests enter from the edges, targeting "Objective Plants".
   - The player (Static Gardener) uses mouse-aimed tools and automated plant-towers to destroy pests.
   - Pests drop "Seeds" (XP/Currency) on death.
   - Collecting Seeds triggers mid-wave "Mutation Choices" (3 random upgrades).
2. **Growth (Strategic):**
   - Victory is achieved when all Objective Plants reach their target level.
   - Growth is driven by: **Time**, **Feeding** (spending collected resources), and **Synergy** (placement near boosters).
3. **Research (Meta):**
   - Successfully harvested plants yield "Research Points".
   - Between waves, players spend Research Points to unlock new plant varieties, better tools, and permanent stat buffs.

## 3. The "Chaos" System
Every wave has a 30% chance of a "Climate Shift" or "Wildcard Event":
- **Shade:** -25% Growth speed (Time).
- **Humidity:** +100% Growth speed (Time).
- **Mutant Squirrel:** Boss pest with high HP and plant-destroying abilities.
- **Garden Tornado:** Relocates 2 random towers and scatters uncollected resources.

## 4. Master Inventory (Initial List)

### A. Plant Varieties (Towers & Objectives)
- **Pea-Shooter (Basic):** Fires high-velocity seeds. Decapitates smaller pests.
- **Venus Chainsaw:** Melee plant that shreds nearby pests into green goo.
- **Sunflower (Objective/Booster):** Generates Sun Resources; boosts growth of nearby plants.
- **Thorn-Vine (Defensive):** Slows pests and deals bleed damage.
- **Acid-Berry:** Shoots projectiles that melt pest armor.

### B. Tools (Active Gardener Equipment)
- **Pesticide Sprayer:** Mouse-aimed cone of poison damage.
- **The Heavy Shovel:** High-knockback manual attack.
- **Watering Can:** Manually injects "Water" resource into Objective Plants for burst growth.
- **The Rake:** Placeable trap that impales ground-based pests.

### C. Pests (Enemies)
- **Aphid Swarm:** Small, fast, easy to pop.
- **Lumbering Beetle:** Tanky, eats plants slowly but surely.
- **Locust Cloud:** Flying pests that bypass ground traps.
- **The Snails:** Slow, but leave a trail that prevents new planting.

### D. Resources & Currencies
- **Seeds:** Collected from kills. Used for mid-wave level-ups.
- **Water/Sun:** Dropped by specific pests or generated by plants. Used to "Feed" growth.
- **Research Points:** Earned from winning waves. Used for permanent unlocks.

## 5. Technical Approach
- **Platform:** Web (React + TypeScript + Canvas/Phaser for game engine).
- **Input:** Mouse-centric (Aiming, Clicking to Feed/Plant).
- **Persistence:** LocalStorage for meta-progress (Research).
