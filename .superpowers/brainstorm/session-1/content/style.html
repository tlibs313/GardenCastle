What's the vibe of our Garden Defense?

The visual style will influence how the "pests" and "towers" look.

A

Whimsical & Bright

Think "Plants vs. Zombies" - vibrant colors, cute but pesky bugs, and animated plant "towers" (pea shooters, sunflowers).

B

Gritty / Realistic Macro

A "Ground Bound" feel. Highly detailed insects (giant beetles, spiders) and realistic garden tools/traps as defenses.

C

Magical / Ethereal

Glowing flora, mystical pests (shadow sprites, corrupted wisps), and arcane garden structures.

D

Classic Pixel Art

Retro 16-bit style. Charming sprites, clear grid-based layout, and a nostalgic arcade feel.
