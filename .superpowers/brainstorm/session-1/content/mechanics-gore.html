How does "The Gardener" play?

This defines the core game loop.

A

The Commander (Static)

Classic TD. You have a bird's-eye view. You place plants/traps on a grid and watch the carnage. You don't have a physical avatar on the field.

B

The Active Gardener (Action-TD)

You control a character (The Gardener) moving around the garden. You manually plant things, but you can also jump in with a shovel or pesticide spray to finish off pests yourself.
