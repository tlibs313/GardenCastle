Objective Plant Varieties

Harvesting these wins the wave. Each has unique needs and rewards.

🌹

The Royal Rose

Need: High Water intake.
Harvest: Unlocks "Elegant" cosmetic castle upgrades and high Research Points.

🌳

Iron Oak Sapling

Need: Extremely slow growth; needs Synergy from defensive plants.
Harvest: Increases Castle HP permanently.

🍄

Lunar Glow-Shroom

Need: Grows faster during "Shade" events.
Harvest: Unlocks "Bioluminescent" tool upgrades (night vision/extra range).

🐉

Fire-Breathing Snap-Dragon

Need: Feeding "Sun" resources exclusively.
Harvest: Becomes a powerful automated turret for the *next* wave.
