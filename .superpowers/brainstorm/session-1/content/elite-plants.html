The "Great Harvest" Candidates

High-value plants worth the maximum protection effort.

🔥

Phoenix Lily

Condition: Only grows in Ash Soil. Needs high Nitrogen additives.
Value: High Research Points; unlocks Fire-based tool upgrades.

🌵

Crystal Cactus

Condition: Only grows in Sandy Soil. Needs 0 water but high Sunlight.
Value: Unlocks "Reflective Armor" boosters for all plants.

🍀

Diamond Clover

Condition: Needs Rich Dirt and 3 different "Complimentary Plants" nearby.
Value: Massive Currency drop; "Insane" difficulty objective.

🥀

Obsidian Orchid

Condition: Only grows in Rock Soil. Needs Shade and "Bone Meal" additives.
Value: Unlocks "Poison Cloud" tower mutation.

More Exotic Ideas?

  • Echo-Fern: Grows faster for every kill made within its radius.
  • Clockwork Sunflower: A hybrid that generates resources and defensive gears.
  • Gravity-Vine: Protect this to keep flyers (Locusts) grounded in its area.
