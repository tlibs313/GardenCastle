Tools & Structures: How to Get Them

Deciding between immediate purchase vs. long-term research.

🛒 The Shop (Seeds)

Ready to buy as soon as you have the currency. Good for consumables and basic items.

  • Basic Pesticide: Standard ammo for sprayer.
  • Wooden Fencing: Temporary barriers.
  • Potting Soil: One-time patch of rich dirt.

🔬 Research (Research Points)

Requires specialized points from successful harvests. Unlocks high-tech or magical versions.

  • Industrial Sprayer: Permanent upgrade to spray range.
  • Iron Greenhouse: Permanent structural HP boost.
  • Automated Irrigation: Unlocks the "Sprinkler" structure.

New Inventory: Structures

Static objects that aren't plants, but support the garden.

🚿

Automatic Sprinkler

Periodically waters all plants in a 3x3 area. Essential for high-water objectives.

💡

UV Lamp Post

Provides "Sunlight" to plants even during Night waves or Shade events.

🧱

Reinforced Wall

Pure defense. High HP barrier that pests must chew through to reach the garden.
