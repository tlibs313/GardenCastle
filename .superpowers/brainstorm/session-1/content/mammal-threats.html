The Yard Marauders: Mammals & Birds

Larger threats with complex movement and thieving behaviors.

🐿️

Acrobatic Squirrel

Property: Verticality. Can jump over Fences and run along Irrigation lines. Targets Seeds first.

🐇

The Speedster Rabbit

Property: Extreme Speed. Bursts across the yard in zig-zags. High damage to Objective Plants if they reach them.

🐦‍⬛

Thieving Crow

Property: Aerial Snatch. Dives down to steal Tools or unharvested Objective Plants. Needs Scarecrows to deter.

🐾

Tunneling Mole

Property: Subterranean. Moves under the grid, destroying **Roots** (de-buffing growth). Only vulnerable to **Heavy Shovel** shockwaves.

Mammal/Bird Interaction Mechanics

  • Distraction: Toss Birdseed or Carrots to temporarily lure these larger pests away from your garden.
  • Trapping: Use the Rake or Havahart Cage structures to capture and "relocate" them (earning high Research Points).
  • Weight Classes: Unlike bugs, these pests aren't always "popped." They might retreat if they take enough damage, dropping loot as they flee.
