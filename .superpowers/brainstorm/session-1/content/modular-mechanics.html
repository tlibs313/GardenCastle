Mechanics: The "Modular Garden"

Inspired by Mech Assemble: Roguelike upgrades for your central defense.

Game Loop: Roguelike Tower Defense

1. Kill Pests

Defeated pests explode into Seeds (XP/Currency).

2. Level Up

Collect enough Seeds to trigger a Mutation Choice.

3. Customize

Add new modular parts (Thorn Sprayers, Acid Roots) to your Garden.

🏰

Your Castle / Core
Static position. You use the mouse to aim its active weapons
and place defensive plants around it.

The Modular Choice

How do we handle the "Mech Assemble" modularity?

A

Fixed Slots (The Castle Tower)

The Castle has specific "hardpoints" (North, South, East, West). You equip specific plant-weapons to these slots and upgrade them individually.

B

The Spreading Garden (Grid-based)

As you level up, you don't just upgrade the castle; you plant new towers on a grid. You choose *where* they go, creating a custom defensive layout every run.
