The Growth-based Defense Loop

Winning isn't just about surviving; it's about successfully harvesting.

Game Flow: Tactical + Strategic + Research

🛡️ Tactical (The Wave)

  • Protect: Stop pests from eating your "Objective Plants".
  • Modular: Level up mid-wave to add weapons to the Castle or buff the Garden.
  • Tools: Use your mouse (The Gardener) to aim active sprays/traps.

🌱 Strategic (The Goal)

  • Grow: Nurture specific plants to Level X to end the wave.
  • Harvest: Successful growth yields "Research Points".
  • Expand: Use grid slots for both defense and objective crops.

🔬 The Research Center (Meta)

Between waves, spend Research Points on: Hardier Stems (Plant HP), Toxic Sap (Auto-damage), Industrial Pesticide (Tool Buffs), New Seeds (Tower Variety).

The "Objective" Mechanics

How do you actually "grow" the plants mid-battle?

A

Time/Survival

Plants grow automatically as long as they aren't being eaten. Protect them for 3 minutes to hit the goal.

B

Resource Injection

You must collect "Water" or "Sunlight" from defeated pests and manually "feed" the objective plants to level them up.

C

Synergy Growth

Defensive plants placed *near* objective plants boost their growth speed. Building a good garden ecosystem is key.
