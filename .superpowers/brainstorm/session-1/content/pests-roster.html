The Pest Gallery: Tactical Threats

Inspired by the varied enemy properties of Bloons TD.

🪲

Iron-Clad Beetle

Property: Armored (Like Lead Bloons). Immune to basic seeds. Needs Acid-Berry or Heavy Shovel to crack.

🦗

Camo-Locust

Property: Stealth. Invisible to automated towers unless a Sensor Array or UV Lamp is nearby.

🐌

Regen-Slug

Property: Regenerative. Heals HP over time. Needs high DPS or Pruning Shears to finish off quickly.

🐜

Splitting Ants

Property: Cluster (Like Black/White Bloons). When popped, releases 3 smaller, faster Aphids.

Environmental Disasters (Wave-Changers)

  • Flash Flood: Briefly washes away ground traps and uncollected seeds. Drowns non-aquatic pests.
  • Hail Storm: Randomly damages both plants AND pests. Forces you to use the Shovel to deflect large chunks.
  • Acid Rain: Slowly erodes plant HP. Requires Vitamin Solution (Watering Can upgrade) to neutralize.
  • The Frost: Freezes all growth and slows pest movement by 80% for 15 seconds.

The Bosses (The "BAD" equivalents)

  • The Mutant Squirrel: Can dig underground to bypass front-line defenses.
  • Goliath Worm: A massive tunneling threat that emerges directly under your Objective plants.
  • Swarm Queen: Doesn't attack directly but continuously spawns Camo-Locusts until destroyed.
