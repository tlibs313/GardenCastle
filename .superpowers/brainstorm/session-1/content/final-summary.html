GardenCastle: Final Design Summary

A comprehensive view of your "Whimsical Splatter" Tower Defense.

The Three Pillars of Your Garden

1. Objective

Protect & Grow. Non-offensive. Provide powerful boosts (e.g., Phoenix Lily, Crystal Cactus).

2. Defensive

Slow & Buff. Complementary plants that shield objectives (e.g., Thorn-Vine, Wall-Nut).

3. Offensive

Kill & Pop. Primary damage dealers (e.g., Pea-Shooter, Venus Chainsaw).

Difficulty Scaling (Objectives per Wave)

Easy: 1 Med: 2 Hard: 3 Insane: 5
